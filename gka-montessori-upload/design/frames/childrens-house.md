# Empowering young minds to

#### play, learn, and grow

AMI-Accredited Montessori School in St. Paul

## A lifelong love of learning begins in the Children’s House

Children’s House helps your child develop the tools they need to lead their own learning. As an AMI-accredited Montessori school, we encourage children to seek knowledge, explore their curiosities, sharpen their focus, and build community.

To allow ample time for exploration and learning, we follow a five-day week schedule through half-day and full-day programming. With a prepared environment designed for their developmental needs, your child will thrive as they joyfully discover the world around them.

## Half-Day Programs

![Child working independently with Montessori materials in morning light](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/f77f5472-a84f-41fe-afa2-47fa50b59959/mvms-morning-half-day-program.jpg)

**Morning Half-Day Program**

**_(8:30 am to 11:30 am)_**

Each morning is a buzz of purposeful activity and social interaction as children work with energy, concentration, and joy. While engaging in independent learning, your child will freely choose activities based on their interests and seize opportunities to teach other children what they have learned.

- More Information

As children arrive between 8:20-8:30 am, they will greet their friends, Guides, and staff.  Throughout the morning, children choose work that builds practical life skills while engaging in sensorial learning, mathematics, language, art, music, geography, and biology. The Guide presents new activities to the children individually or in small groups, helping children learn to cooperate, assist others, and resolve conflicts.

Children can also choose restful activities, such as reading a book, talking to friends, or having a snack, as they feel the need. At the end of the morning cycle, each child completes their classroom work, which helps to make sure their environment is organized and prepared. At 11:30 am, the morning children leave our West St. Paul preschool for the day.

![Children playing outdoors in nature during lunch and recess time](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/78d418fa-48f6-4345-9c89-a699612b6f64/mvms-lunch-and-play-outdoor-time.jpg)

**Lunch & Play Program**

**_(11:30 am to 1:00 pm)_**

Following the morning work cycle, children are invited to explore nature and play outdoors, rain or shine. With direct access to our vast backyard area and gardens, they will have an abundance of space to roam, run, and play with their peers before lunchtime.

- More Information

When the weather permits, the children love going outdoors to play in our beautiful backyard until 12:20 pm. While outdoors, they can engage in a variety of open-ended play, including riding bikes, sledding, digging in our sand pit, and engaging in imaginative play. In case of inclement weather, we also have access to a private, on-site indoor gym.

At 12:20 pm, children return to their classrooms to eat with the Guides and their peers. Lunch is a leisurely time for conversation and nourishment. We encourage parents to provide low-processed, balanced food options. After lunch, the children wash their dishes and clean up. Children enrolled in Lunch & Play leave for the day at 1 pm.

## Full-Day Programs

![nap area with soft lighting and calm music](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/fa29b9ee-4a23-4366-a327-8c4fde08456b/mvms-full-day-napping.jpg)

**FULL-Day Program**

**_NAPPING_**

**_(8:30 am to 3:00 pm)_**

During the full-day program, some children may need a dedicated nap period. Since sleep is paramount to a child’s development, we emphasize midday naps for any children who will benefit, complete with story time, tranquil music, and a peaceful environment.

- More Information

Our nap program is a time of establishing sleep routines. Your child will be comforted as they read stories and listen to light music before napping. However, this time is flexible enough to include children who may need to occasionally rest after a busy morning. At 1 pm, children who take naps do so in the nap room.

![Child engaging in afternoon Montessori work outdoors](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/a76d5cbe-2c49-4521-9c17-6f8338e75770/mvms-afternoon-second-work-cycle.jpg)

**Full-Day Program**

**_Second Work Cycle_**

**_(8:30 am to 3:00 pm)_**

Non-napping children spend their afternoons in a second work cycle, diving back into independent learning and collaborative small group lessons with enthusiasm and wonder. We also encourage outdoor work cycles during the afternoon, giving children substantial time to explore our natural surroundings.

- More Information

We have a beautiful, expansive yard with raised garden beds, a rain garden, and a shaded large group area. We also have a wooded area with access to a natural pond. Our West St. Paul preschool’s proximity to Dodge Nature Center means we can take advantage of the many trails, farm animals, pond docks, reptile rescue center, and woods.
