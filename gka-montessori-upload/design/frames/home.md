# Preparing your child for life

AMI-Accredited WEST St. Paul Montessori School

[Schedule Your Tour](https://www.mvmontessori.org/contact)

## Where children become their best selves

![Brand Watermark design](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/67332eb0-e1ce-4b05-92a1-db640e654a81/hopeful-curious-child-montessori.jpg)

Children thrive when they direct their own education. We help children cultivate a lifelong love of learning through child-centered Montessori programs.

MVMS has a rich history in serving children from 33 months to 6 years old in our beautifully prepared environment. Since 1967, it’s been our honor to prepare children for life, not just first grade.

[Learn More](https://www.mvmontessori.org/about)

## Why Choose MVMS

![Authentic Montessori materials supporting early childhood development at MVMS](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/0cc828d4-3c6e-4810-bf94-5d6e6b987ebf/montessori-materials-ami-education.jpg)

**Authentic Montessori Experience**

As an AMI-accredited school, we provide authentic Montessori learning experiences that focus on developing the whole child.

![Montessori student independently working with learning materials in a peaceful classroom environment](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/e044cf33-4aa6-4105-a29e-d0c7b764262e/montessori-student-independent-learning.jpg)

**Child-Led  Education**

Children thrive when they have the freedom to choose which materials to engage with, giving them a sense of purpose and independence.

![Montessori experience with indoor-outdoor learning and AMI-trained guides](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/d345752a-206c-46b8-b050-cee6e1dd4a3b/montessori-experience-ami.jpg)

**Indoor & Outdoor Exploration**

We are a hidden gem in West St. Paul, known for our thoughtfully designed classrooms that connect to our vast outdoor space.

## AMI-Accredited Montessori School

A pioneer of early childhood education, Dr. Maria Montessori founded the AMI to protect and preserve her methodology. As the second oldest Montessori School in MN, we are proud to provide an authentic Montessori learning environment that meets the AMI’s rigorous standards.

[Why Montessori](https://www.mvmontessori.org/montessori)
