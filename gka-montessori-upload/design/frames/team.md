# Meet the Team

At Mississippi Valley Montessori School, our staff believes in nurturing your child’s love of learning. From the Guides who help in our classrooms to the support staff who make our programs run smoothly, we all come together to provide an authentic Montessori environment where your child can thrive.

Leading with care, respect, and kindness, our staff sets the tone for your child’s early education, helping them develop into leaders within their communities.

**_Scroll to learn more about each of our incredible MVMS staff!_**

- ![Headshot of Shalene Jahn, Head of School at MVMS](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/28688aa7-bd76-4b5d-bf4e-7cbd854fad16/Ms._Shalene_01.jpg)

## Shalene Jahn

**_Head of School_**

It's my pleasure to serve as the Head of School of Mississippi Valley Montessori School, a place that has been a significant part of my life since I was a child. I hold a BS degree in Health Care Administration & Business Administration, a certificate in Supervisory Management, and my AMI Montessori 3-6 Orientation Course Certification. My connection to MVMS runs deep; I was a student here myself, and both of my children also attended from ages 3-6. What I love most about MVMS is its unique ability to truly value the whole child, the whole teacher/staff person, and to provide a loving, nurturing community for everyone. Our school creates a profound sense of belonging, and anyone who has been part of our community always has a home here. When I'm not at MVMS, I enjoy cooking, gardening, building community, and spending time with friends and family.

## Ms. Bailey

**_Assistant Head of School_**

I'm a Pennsylvania native who first moved to the Twin Cities to attend Macalester College, where I studied Education and Sociology. I joined the staff of MVMS as a classroom assistant in 2015. Since that first experience in a Montessori environment, I've spent the past 10 years in the classroom, both in Oregon and Minnesota.

I returned to MVMS in 2019 after completing my AMI Primary Montessori diploma and Masters degree at Washington Montessori Institute and Loyola University Maryland.

In my free time, I love to be outside -- hiking, biking, camping, gardening, and paddling. I also love to quilt & bake!

I feel so lucky to be part of this school community, which is so beautifully full of deep connections. My favorite part of working at MVMS is how often alumni students & families come back to visit!

## Ms. Liz

**_Substitute_**

Working with children has always been a passion of mine. After raising three children of my own as a stay-at-home mom, I started working at MVMS.
