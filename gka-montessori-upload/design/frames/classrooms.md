Dr. Maria Montessori believed that "to assist a child, we must provide \[them\] with an environment which will enable \[them\] to develop freely." This is why every facet of our environment fosters a sense of spontaneity within an orderly framework.

From our expansive outdoor spaces to our carefully curated classrooms, your child will delight in a child-led education rooted in respect, harmony, and community.

## Our Classroom Features

**Child-Led Learning**

With purposefully arranged materials, our classrooms encourage children to engage in their interests at their own pace.

**Order & Organization**

Children thrive in uncluttered, tidy environments that minimize distractions, helping them refine their focus and attention.

**Harmony & Unity**

Our mixed-age classrooms instill a sense of peace and belonging, supporting children as they develop social and emotional skills.

**Beautiful Quality Materials**

Our natural materials inspire your child to explore their curiosities while developing their concentration and confidence.

**Child-First Design**

All of our counters, tables, shelves, and chairs are at a child’s level, promoting independence and freedom.

**Hands-On Environment**

Hands-on learning helps children actively engage with materials, nature, and real-world experiences.

## Our Outdoor Space

#### Exploring

Unique to our school, we are happily surrounded by nature and encourage children to be outside daily. With direct access to the sprawling acreage, children love to explore our vast backyard area.

A short walk from Dodge Nature Center’s trailhead and Weschcke Park, we often take walking field trips. This helps us practice routines and build observation and listening skills—all while engaging in imaginative play.

- Read More

Surrounded by mature pine trees, our outdoor space features:

  - A rain garden
  - A raised garden with flower beds
  - A natural sand area surrounded by boulders
  - A large grassy area for running and yard games
  - A paved area for bikes, trikes, and scooters
  - A shady pergola for group work
  - A composting area and worm farm
  - An outdoor chalkboard
  - A water tower and a water trough system

We also provide rain suits for all children, so we can spend time outdoors daily. Whether your child wants to engage in nature, learn about the plant growth cycle, or simply wants space to roam and play, it’s all possible at MVMS.

#### Explore
