# Flexible care options for all families

Offering extended day Preschool & Kindergarten programs

![setting up classroom activities in the early morning](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/1747942793765-26EW33L9FJZ3TQOFKOPL/mvms-before-care-preparation.jpg)

Before Care

In the morning, children play an active role in helping us prepare for the day. Your child will have access to our quality materials, which include books, crafts, activities, games, and more.

![Child enjoying art activity after school](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/30bf1022-517b-46e6-a809-5a49895694e8/mvms-after-care-outdoor-activity.jpg)

AFTER Care

In the after-school program, children share a snack before seeking out individual or group activities inside or outdoors. We emphasize exploring art, literacy, and nature during this time of day. Children may also help in taking care of their Children’s House classroom.

![Montessori student playing quietly in a prepared environment during drop-in care](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/681392becab3437a235aa6b5/03741c3f-744e-4e67-86af-ca6a524eaa0a/mvms-drop-in-care-flexible-support.jpg)

Drop-In Care

With prior notice, drop-in care services are available for children before and after school. Flexible care is important to us at MVMS. This helps families if a caregiver has a meeting, appointment, or any need for additional care.
